# Plans and pricing

Acme Notes has three plans.

## Free

For trying Acme Notes out. Up to 3 notebooks, 100 MB of storage and one
member. No invoices, because there is nothing to pay.

## Pro

For one person who writes a lot. Unlimited notebooks, 10 GB of storage,
version history and offline access. Pro costs $12.00 a month, billed monthly.

## Team

For groups working in shared notebooks. Everything in Pro, plus shared
notebooks, admin controls and 50 GB of storage per seat. Team costs
$120.00 per seat a year, billed annually for every seat on the account.
