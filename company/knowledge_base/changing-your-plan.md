# Changing your plan

You can move between Free, Pro and Team at any time.

## Upgrading

An upgrade takes effect straight away, and the new plan's limits apply at
once. The first invoice for the new plan is dated the day you upgrade.

## Downgrading

A downgrade also takes effect straight away. If you now have more notebooks
than the new plan allows, nothing is deleted: the extra notebooks become
read-only until you remove some or upgrade again.

A downgrade does not refund the old plan on its own. See the refund policy for
what can be refunded, and ask for it when you downgrade.
