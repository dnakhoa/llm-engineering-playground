# Refund policy

Acme Notes refunds the part of a paid plan you have not used yet. This policy
covers every paid plan, monthly or annual.

## The refund window

You can ask for a refund on an invoice for up to 30 days after the invoice
date. After 30 days, that invoice cannot be refunded at all, even if most of
its billing period is still ahead. This applies to annual plans too: an annual
plan can be refunded in its first 30 days only.

## How much is refunded: proration

A refund covers the unused days of the invoice's billing period:

    refund = amount paid x unused days / days in the billing period

Unused days are the days in the billing period minus the days since the
invoice date. The result is rounded down to the cent. Anything already
refunded on the same invoice is taken off.

Example: a $30.00 monthly invoice (30 days) refunded 10 days after its date
has 20 unused days, so the refund is $30.00 x 20 / 30 = $20.00.

## The maximum refund

No single refund can be more than $200.00, whatever proration gives. A larger
refund is handled by the Acme Notes billing team, not by support.

## Downgrades and cancellations

Downgrading or cancelling does not refund anything by itself. If you downgrade
or cancel inside the refund window, ask for the prorated refund of the current
invoice at the same time.
